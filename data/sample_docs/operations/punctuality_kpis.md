# Punctuality and Reliability KPIs

MetroLink Transit tracks service quality through three core indicators,
reviewed monthly by the operations committee.

## On-time performance (OTP)
A departure is on time when it leaves within 3 minutes of schedule. Network
target: 92% monthly OTP. Lines below 88% for two consecutive months trigger a
dedicated action plan.

## Kilometers operated vs planned
The ratio of commercial kilometers actually operated against the contractual
plan. Target: 99.2%. Cancelled kilometers are categorized by cause: staffing,
rolling stock, external events.

## First and last departure compliance
First and last departures of the day are contractually binding and tracked
separately: a missed first departure counts as a critical event and is
reported to the transport authority within 48 hours.
