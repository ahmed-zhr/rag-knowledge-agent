# Service Disruption Response Procedure

When a service disruption occurs on any MetroLink Transit line, the duty
operations manager applies the following escalation levels.

## Level 1 - Minor delay (under 10 minutes)
Passenger information screens are updated automatically. No manual action is
required beyond logging the event in the daily operations report.

## Level 2 - Significant delay (10 to 30 minutes)
The duty manager activates the passenger notification workflow: platform
announcements every 5 minutes, mobile app alert, and coordination with the
adjacent lines to absorb transfer passengers. A replacement bus is placed on
standby if the delay affects a terminus station.

## Level 3 - Line interruption (over 30 minutes)
A replacement bus service is activated within 20 minutes on the affected
segment. The operations control center notifies the network coordination desk,
and the incident commander opens a live incident channel. Passenger
compensation rules apply after 45 minutes of interruption.

## Reporting
Every Level 2 and Level 3 event requires an incident report within 24 hours,
filed by the duty manager and reviewed weekly by the operations committee.
