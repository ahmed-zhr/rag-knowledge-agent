# Breakdown Priority Matrix

Reported defects are triaged into four priorities by the maintenance
coordinator.

## P1 - Safety-critical (immediate withdrawal)
Brake defects, steering anomalies, door safety failures. The vehicle is
withdrawn from service immediately and towed if necessary. Repair starts before
any other work in the shop.

## P2 - Service-critical (fix within 24 hours)
Defects that degrade service without safety impact: HVAC failure, destination
display outage, ticket validator failure on more than half of the doors.

## P3 - Comfort (fix within one week)
Single validator outage, interior lighting, seat damage without sharp edges,
minor bodywork.

## P4 - Cosmetic (next scheduled maintenance)
Scratches, faded markings, non-structural trim. Bundled with the next Cycle B
visit to avoid extra immobilization.
