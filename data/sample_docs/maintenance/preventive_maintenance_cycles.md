# Preventive Maintenance Cycles - Bus Fleet

MetroLink Transit applies a distance-based preventive maintenance program to
its bus fleet.

## Cycle A - every 5,000 km
Safety inspection: brakes, tires, lights, door mechanisms, driver cabin
controls. Duration: 2 hours. Performed at the depot overnight, no vehicle
immobilization during service hours.

## Cycle B - every 20,000 km
Cycle A plus fluid replacement, filter changes, suspension check, and
diagnostic readout of the engine control unit. Duration: half a day.

## Cycle C - every 60,000 km
Full technical inspection including transmission service, cooling system
overhaul and corrosion inspection. Duration: 2 days. Vehicles are scheduled
out of service and replaced from the reserve fleet.

## Reserve fleet rule
The depot maintains a reserve rate of 8% of the operated fleet to cover Cycle C
immobilizations and unplanned repairs without impacting service.
