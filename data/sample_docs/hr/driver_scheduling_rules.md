# Driver Scheduling Rules

Rosters for MetroLink Transit drivers follow these binding rules, negotiated
with staff representatives.

## Daily limits
Maximum driving time: 9 hours per day, extendable to 10 hours at most twice a
week. Minimum daily rest: 11 consecutive hours, reducible to 9 hours three
times a week with compensating rest.

## Breaks
A 45-minute break is mandatory after 4.5 hours of driving. It can be split into
a 15-minute and a 30-minute break, in that order.

## Weekly organization
Maximum 6 consecutive working days. Weekly rest of at least 45 hours every
second week, and never less than 24 consecutive hours.

## Roster publication
Monthly rosters are published at least 10 days before the start of the month.
Shift swaps between drivers require coordinator approval no later than 48 hours
before the shift.
