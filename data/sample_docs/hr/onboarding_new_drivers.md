# Onboarding Program - New Drivers

Every new driver completes a 4-week onboarding program before operating in
commercial service.

## Week 1 - Company and safety fundamentals
Company presentation, safety culture, emergency procedures, and the
zero-alcohol policy. Ends with a written safety assessment (pass mark: 80%).

## Weeks 2-3 - Line training
Supervised driving on the assigned lines with a certified instructor driver:
route knowledge, regulation points, terminus maneuvers and passenger
interaction standards.

## Week 4 - Evaluation and certification
Independent driving under observation, final evaluation covering punctuality,
smoothness, procedure compliance and customer attitude. Certification is
granted by the training manager and recorded in the driver's file.

## Mentorship
Each new driver is paired with a mentor for the first 3 months, with a check-in
meeting at 30 and 90 days.
